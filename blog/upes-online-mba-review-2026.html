UPES Online MBA 2026 — Overview

UPES (University of Petroleum and Energy Studies), Dehradun offers an online MBA through its UPES Online division. NAAC A+ accredited. WES recognised. Fee: approximately ₹1,10,000 total — one of the more affordable NAAC A+ online MBA options. UPES has a strong physical campus in Dehradun and built its reputation in energy, oil & gas, and engineering sectors before expanding into business programmes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPES Online MBA valid?

Yes — UPES Online MBA is UGC-DEB approved, NAAC A+ accredited, and WES recognised. It is a valid degree for employment, higher studies, and immigration. UPES is a recognised private university with strong brand in energy and engineering sectors.

UPES or Manipal Online MBA — which is better?

Manipal MUJ (NAAC A+, ₹1.35L, national brand, Google Cloud cert, 10+ specialisations) has stronger national recognition. UPES (NAAC A+, ₹1.10L, Energy Management specialisation) is better for energy sector professionals and is more affordable. For most general career purposes: Manipal has stronger brand. For energy/oil/gas/power sector: UPES's Energy Management specialisation is unique.

What is UPES Online MBA fee in 2026?

UPES Online MBA total fee: approximately ₹1,10,000 over 2 years. This is among the most affordable NAAC A+ online MBA options in India alongside SMU (Sikkim Manipal University, NAAC A++). Verify exact current fee structure at time of application.
