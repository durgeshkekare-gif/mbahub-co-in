IIM Nagpur Blended MBA — Total Fee 2026

Total programme fee: ₹12,10,000 (exclusive of GST at 18%). All-in total including GST: approximately ₹14,27,800. Application fee: ₹2,500 + GST (separate, non-refundable).

GST on IIM Nagpur Blended MBA

Educational services from IIM institutions attract 18% GST. GST of 18% applies on the ₹12,10,000 programme fee — adding approximately ₹2,17,800. Total all-in: approximately ₹14,27,800. GST is charged on each instalment at 18%. Check with your employer — if paying through company sponsorship, Input Tax Credit (ITC) may be claimable.

How the Fee Compares to Regular IIM MBA

A full-time MBA at IIM Ahmedabad costs ₹25-30L (plus 2 years foregone salary of ~₹15-30L = total ₹40-60L). IIM Nagpur Blended MBA at ₹12.10L + GST (~₹14.28L) with zero income loss is dramatically more cost-effective for professionals earning ₹8L+ annually.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total fee for IIM Nagpur Blended MBA 2026?

Total programme fee: ₹12,10,000 excluding GST. With 18% GST: approximately ₹14,27,800. Application fee (non-refundable): ₹2,500 + GST. Payment is in 4 instalments over 2 years. Education loans available from SBI, HDFC Credila, Axis Bank.

Can I get a refund if I withdraw from IIM Nagpur Blended MBA?

Refund policy varies by stage of withdrawal. The ₹2,500 application fee is non-refundable. The ₹1,00,000 offer acceptance fee is typically non-refundable. For subsequent instalments, refund terms depend on the date of withdrawal — verify IIM Nagpur's current refund policy at time of application.

Is IIM Nagpur MBA fee paid in one go or in instalments?

In instalments over 2 years. The structure is: ₹1L at offer acceptance, then 4 instalments spread across the 4 semesters (approximately ₹1.99L, ₹3.06L, ₹2.99L, ₹3.06L). Monthly EMI is available through education loan providers — approximating ₹50,000/month on a 24-month repayment.
