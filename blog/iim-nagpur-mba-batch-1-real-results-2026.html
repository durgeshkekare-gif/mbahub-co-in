Why the Blended MBA Produces Different Outcomes Than Online MBAs

Online MBA from Manipal, Amrita or Symbiosis costs Rs 1-1.5L and gives a valid university degree. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA costs Rs 12.10L and gives a government IIM MBA degree. The outcomes documented in Batch 1 — the specific career changes, salary increases and role upgrades — reflect the IIM institutional credential weight, not just any MBA degree.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Nagpur Blended MBA better than online MBA from Manipal?

They are categorically different products. Online MBA from Manipal (Rs 1.35L) gives a NAAC A+ university degree — strong for general employment. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (Rs 12.10L) gives a government IIM MBA degree — different shortlisting category for senior roles. Batch 1 success stories document the specific career outcomes the IIM credential produces, which an online MBA typically does not achieve.

What salary increase did Batch 1 professionals get?

IIM Nagpur has documented Batch 1 outcomes including salary increases. The range varies significantly by industry, experience level, and how actively participants used the credential. Read the Batch 1 success story documentation on the Jaro Education website for specific outcome data — it is the most accurate available resource.

Is IIM Nagpur MBA good for fresh MBA aspirants?

IIM Nagpur Blended MBA requires minimum 3 years work experience — it is not a fresh graduate programme. For working professionals with 3-20 years of experience who cannot do a full-time campus MBA, it is the best IIM MBA degree option available in India.
