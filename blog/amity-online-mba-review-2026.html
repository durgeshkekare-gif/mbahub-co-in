Amity Online MBA 2026 — Overview

Amity University Online MBA is a 2-year programme offered through Amity Online, the distance and online arm of Amity University (Noida, Delhi NCR). NAAC A+ accredited. WES recognised. Fee: Rs 1,35,000 total. Amity's primary strength is its North India brand presence and the largest private university alumni network in India — over 2 lakh alumni across campuses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Amity Online MBA valid?

Yes — Amity Online MBA is UGC-DEB approved, NAAC A+ accredited and WES recognised. It is a valid MBA degree for employment, higher studies and immigration purposes. Amity University is a recognised private university with strong North India presence.

Amity or Manipal — which online MBA is better?

Manipal MUJ (NAAC A+, 10+ specialisations, Google Cloud cert, strongest national brand) vs Amity (NAAC A+, largest private alumni network, strongest North India brand). For pan-India career: Manipal. For North India career and alumni network size: Amity. For best accreditation: Amrita (NAAC A++).

What is Amity Online MBA fee in 2026?

Amity Online MBA total fee: approximately Rs 1,35,000 over 2 years. Paid in semester-wise instalments of approximately Rs 33,750 per semester. Verify current fees at time of application as fee structure may be updated annually.
