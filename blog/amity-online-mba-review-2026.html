August 10, 2026 · 6 min read · mbahub.co.in

Amity Online MBA — Overview

Amity Online MBA is offered by Amity University Online, the digital education arm of Amity University. NAAC A+ accredited. WES recognised. Fee approximately ₹1.35L total. Multiple specialisations including Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and IT. 2-year programme with live weekend sessions.

Amity Online MBA — Strengths

  • WES recognised — valuable for Canada PR and international credential evaluation
  • Strong alumni network — Amity has large alumni base, particularly strong in Delhi NCR
  • Multiple specialisation options including International Business
  • NAAC A+ accreditation
  • Amity brand is well-known in MSME and family business circles in North India

Amity Online MBA — Limitations

  • NAAC A+ vs Amrita/Symbiosis A++ — accreditation grade is lower than top-2 online MBA options
  • No industry certifications bundled (unlike Amrita's Oracle/Amazon or Manipal's Google Cloud)
  • Brand recognition strongest in Delhi NCR — less prominent in South India or pan-India corporate
  • Placement support variable — outcomes depend heavily on alumni network utilisation

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Amity Online MBA valid in India?

Yes — Amity Online MBA is UGC-DEB approved, NAAC A+ accredited and WES recognised. It is a valid MBA degree for employment and higher education purposes across India.

Is Amity Online MBA good for placement?

Amity provides placement support through its career portal and alumni network. The Amity alumni base is large, especially in Delhi NCR and North India. However, placement outcomes for online programmes depend heavily on individual effort, network activation, and the candidate's own experience and skills.

Amity vs Manipal for Online MBA — which is better?

Manipal MUJ Online MBA (₹1.35L, NAAC A+) has stronger national brand recognition and Google Cloud certification bundled. Amity Online MBA (₹1.35L, NAAC A+) has a stronger alumni network in Delhi NCR and more specialisation options including International Business. Both are at the same fee and NAAC grade — choose based on geography and target sector.
